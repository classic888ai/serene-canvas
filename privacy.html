Serene Canvas — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Serene Canvas is a relaxing paint-by-number app made by PrintableDrops. This policy explains what information the app handles, in plain language.

What we collect

Nothing personal. Serene Canvas has no accounts, no sign-ins, and no forms. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, or location.

What stays on your device

Your painting progress, settings, coins, and boosters are stored only on your device. Photos you import with "New from photo" are converted to a paintable picture entirely on your device — the photo itself is never uploaded anywhere. The Backup feature saves a file you control.

Advertising

Serene Canvas shows banner ads and optional rewarded videos provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as your device's advertising identifier to show and measure ads, as described in Google's privacy & terms. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device's settings. Rewarded videos only play when you choose to watch them.

Analytics

The app keeps a small anonymous event log (for example, "a picture was completed") on your device to help us improve the app. It contains no personal information.

Children

Serene Canvas is designed for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions? Email support@printabledrops.com.
